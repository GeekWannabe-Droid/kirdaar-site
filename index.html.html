FAQ

Kya ye mere existing WhatsApp Business number pe chalega?
Ji bilkul. Aapko naya number ya SIM nahi chahiye. Aik QR scan aur aapka current number Kirdaar se connect ho jata hai — customers ko sirf faster replies milte hain, aur kuch change nahi lagta.
Urdu voice notes kitni real lagti hain?
Hum state-of-the-art voice AI use karte hain jo natural Pakistani Urdu bolti hai — robot wali awaaz nahi. Demo mein khud sun lein, phir faisla karein.
Payment verification kaise kaam karta hai?
Customer bank transfer ka screenshot bhejta hai. Kirdaar us se amount, bank aur transaction details parh kar aapke records se match karta hai. Sahi hui to booking auto-confirm, mashkook hui to aapko alert.
Agar AI se koi aisa sawal ho jaye jo wo handle na kar sake?
Kirdaar gracefully aapko forward kar deta hai aur aapko notify karta hai. Aap kabhi bhi kisi conversation mein khud enter ho sakte hain — number aapka hai, control aapka hai.
Mera customer data kahan store hota hai? Safe hai?
Data encrypted enterprise-grade database mein store hota hai, sirf aapke business ke liye. Hum aapka data kisi ko sell nahi karte — period.
